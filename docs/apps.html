Apps

Everything around the API.

OBP-API comes with ready-made apps for developers, bank staff and operators. Each one logs in through OBP and sees only what its Roles allow, so you can run all of them on day one.

What every app shares

  • One loginPeople log in through OBP with OAuth2 and OpenID Connect, using the identity provider you already run
  • Platform AppsEach app declares the Scopes it needs, and an administrator grants them in API Manager
  • Roles on every callEach person sees and does only what their own Roles allow, checked by OBP-API
  • Ready to watchEvery app has a status page, and serves its logs and Telemetry to OBP Senti
  • Your words, your brandLogos, colours and text come from OBP-API settings, so one change reaches every app
  • Open sourceAGPL, with commercial licences from TESOBE, and a Docker image for each app
