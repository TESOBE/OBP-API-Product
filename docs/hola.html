OBP Hola

See the whole journey, end to end.

OBP Hola is a working third-party app that walks through login, consent, account access and payments, in UK Open Banking, Berlin Group and OBP styles, against your own OBP-API.

One journey, every step

  1. Choose a standardUK Open Banking (3.1 or 4.0), Berlin Group, or OBP's own APIs, including variable recurring payments.
  2. Ask for consentPick the bank, the accounts and the permissions, how often and until when.
  3. Log in and approveThe customer logs in at the bank's identity provider and grants access, with SCA.
  4. Use itRead accounts, balances, transactions and counterparties, and make payments, with the consent.
  5. Revoke itList the consents given and revoke one, and see access end.

What it is for

  • Showing it workingA live demo of open banking from the customer's side, for banks, regulators and partners
  • A reference for developersReadable Java code for each flow, to copy into a real third-party app
  • Testing a platformProve that login, consent, mTLS and data access work on a new installation
  • Automated testsDrives end-to-end browser tests with OBP Selenium

What it covers

  • Three standardsUK Open Banking, Berlin Group and OBP, each with its own consent flow
  • Accounts and paymentsAccounts, balances, transactions and counterparties, and payments to a counterparty, an account or an IBAN
  • Variable recurring paymentsOBP consent requests for VRP, with their limits
  • mTLSCalls OBP-API with a client certificate, and shows the certificate it presented
  • Your identity providerOBP-OIDC by default, or Ory Hydra with private_key_jwt
  • ConsentsEvery consent given, its status and expiry, and revocation

Run it

  • One JAR or one imageJava and Spring Boot, built with Maven, with a Dockerfile for Docker and Kubernetes
  • Configured by environmentThe OBP-API address, the identity provider and the client credentials
  • Your certificatesA keystore and truststore when OBP-API requires mTLS
  • Open sourceAGPL on GitHub, with commercial licences from TESOBE
