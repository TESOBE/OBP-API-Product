Opey

An assistant that asks first.

Opey is the AI assistant inside the Portal, API Manager and every OBP app. It answers questions, finds the right endpoint and acts on banking data, only with the consent each person gives it.

What it does

  • Answers questionsAbout OBP, your APIs, the standards and the glossary, in plain language
  • Finds the right endpointAnd explains what it needs, from more than 600 endpoints
  • Acts on banking dataReads accounts and transactions, and more, through OBP-MCP
  • Fills in forms for youDrafts the values into the form you have open; you check them and submit
  • Built into every appThe Portal, API Manager, Cohort and Conformator share one chat component
  • Streams its answersReplies appear as they are written, and you can stop or regenerate one

Safe by design

  • Consent for every callEach call to OBP-API carries a consent the person approved; the model never sees the token
  • You approve each toolFor one call, for the conversation, or always, and you can batch approvals
  • Your own permissionsOpey can do no more than the person it is helping
  • Open to guests, safelyAnonymous chat with limits, and a login when banking data is needed
  • Rate limits and usageLimits per person and overall, with usage tracked
  • Your choice of modelAnthropic, OpenAI or a local model through Ollama

Run it

  • A Python serviceFastAPI and LangGraph, with Redis, beside your OBP-API
  • Tools from MCPIts banking tools come from OBP-MCP, or any MCP server you add
  • One setting per appEach app points at Opey with one address, and can add its own instructions
  • Open sourceAGPL on GitHub, with commercial licences from TESOBE
