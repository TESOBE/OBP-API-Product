The standards we serve

Open Bank Project

v1.2.1 to v7.0.0v7.0.0

Our own API, and the widest: 900+ endpoints for accounts, payments, customers, products, consents, access control, dynamic APIs and operations. Old versions keep working while new ones are added, so apps upgrade when they choose.

Berlin Group NextGenPSD2

v1.3

The European PSD2 standard: account information, payment initiation, confirmation of funds and signing baskets, with consents and SCA.

UK Open Banking

v3.1v4.0.1

Accounts and transactions, payment initiation, confirmation of funds, event notifications and variable recurring payments, with consent authorisation and FAPI headers.
