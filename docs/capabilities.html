Capabilities

Everything a bank's API needs, in one platform.

From the customer's account to the operator's dashboard: banking, customers, products, assets and your own APIs, all with the same access control and documentation.

Accounts and payments

Accounts

Accounts at many banks behind one API.

  • Balances, account holders and attributes
  • Account applications and access requests
  • Views that decide which fields each party sees

Payments

Payment requests that follow the bank's own rules.

  • SEPA, counterparty, account and free-form payments
  • Challenges, SCA and maker/checker approval
  • Standing orders, direct debits and variable recurring payments

v7.0.0

Utility and bill payments

Pay a bill or buy a prepaid electricity token.

  • Look the payee up first, to confirm who is paid
  • Get the result later by callback

v7.0.0

Payment promises and netting

Hold payments between banks as promises, and settle them together.

  • Each promise carries the payer's originator information
  • One net transaction per pair of banks
  • Fees settled separately

Counterparties

Payees with their own metadata and limits.

  • Counterparty limits per payment, day or month
  • Tags, comments, images and locations on transactions

Foreign exchange

FX rates per bank, and conversion that keeps each currency's own decimal places.

Customers, KYC and files

Customers and KYC

Retail and corporate customers, with everything a bank records about them.

  • KYC checks, documents and statuses
  • Customer attributes, addresses and tax residence
  • Customers linked to Users and to accounts

v7.0.0

Files

Documents and photographs, stored safely and attached to the records they belong to.

  • Content can never be altered once stored
  • Scanned before others can open it
  • Shared with chosen Users, or published

v7.0.0

Customer emails

Send a customer a plain-text email at the address on their record, without the address being revealed to the sender.

Products, cards and places

Products and fees

Accounts, loans, cards and savings products, with their attributes, fees and collections.

Cards

Card records with their attributes, limits and controls.

Branches and ATMs

Locations, opening hours, services and accessibility, searchable by place.

Assets and retention

v7.0.0

The asset registry

Every currency, precious metal and accounting unit in ISO 4217, crypto assets, and the assets a bank issues itself: deposit tokens, stablecoins, bonds and fund shares.

  • Each asset has its own decimal places, from the yen to the Omani rial
  • Banks register their own assets through the API
  • Tokens are linked to their identity on the chain

v7.0.0

Data retention

Retention policies per space say how long records are kept, and whether they are archived or deleted afterwards.

Dynamic APIs

Go beyond the standard banking model without waiting for a release.

Dynamic Entities

Define your own data model in JSON. OBP creates its endpoints and Roles at once.

  • Typed fields, references and field-level Roles
  • Each record says who created and changed it, and for whom

Dynamic Endpoints and Resource Docs

Add endpoints from an OpenAPI document, or write their logic in Scala or Java, without a release.

v7.0.0

Domain APIs

Publish a space's entities and endpoints under a base path of their own, with their own OpenAPI document, so your API is not hidden behind OBP's URLs.

Webhooks

Tell outside systems when records change, with identifiers only, so they read the data with their own permissions.

Endpoint mapping and method routing

Route endpoints and connector methods to the implementation that should answer them.

Spaces

Dynamic data, files and docs live in a space: one bank, or the system space shared by all.

AI agents

Opey

A conversational assistant that answers questions about the API and the user's own data, with the user's own permissions.

OBP-MCP

A Model Context Protocol server, so any AI agent can find the right endpoint and call it.

Agents acting for people

An agent can act on behalf of a User, and every record says both who acted and for whom.

Operations

Telemetry

Requests, connector calls, caches, the database pool and memory, for Prometheus and Grafana.

API metrics

Every call recorded, with its Consumer, User, duration and outcome, and aggregated on demand.

Traffic and deployment checks

See where traffic comes from right now, and check that proxies, rate limits and logging are set up well.
