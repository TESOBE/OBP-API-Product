Architecture

Between your applications and your systems.

Applications call OBP-API. OBP-API applies the standards, authentication, Roles and views, then reaches each bank's systems through the connector that suits them.

How a call flows

Each call is authenticated, checked against the caller's Roles and views, and passed to the connector configured for that bank and method.

Connectors

A connector translates OBP's calls for your systems. Choose one per bank, or one per method.

  • MappedOBP's own database, for sandboxes and new banks
  • RESTCall your systems' own HTTP APIs
  • RabbitMQReliable messages to an adapter beside your core banking system
  • gRPCLow-latency calls to an adapter
  • AkkaRemote calls into a secure zone
  • Stored proceduresStraight into the bank's database
  • Cardano and EthereumAccounts and payments on a blockchain
  • StarRoute each method to a different connector

The OBP ecosystem

API Explorer II

Try every endpoint, read its documentation and see the Roles it needs.

API Manager II

Manage banks, users, Roles, consumers, rate limits and caches.

OBP Portal

Where developers sign up, register their apps and get started.

Opey and OBP-MCP

AI agents that find and call endpoints with the user's own permissions.

OBP-OIDC and Keycloak

Identity providers for development and production.

OBP Senti

Watches running instances and suggests the improvements that matter most.

OBP Bank Node

Runs inside a bank's network and connects it to a platform operator, with outbound connections only.

Adapters

SEPA, RabbitMQ and gRPC adapters that sit beside your core banking system.

OBP-API Dispatch

Routes requests between instances, for example by region.

OBP Cohort

Runs startup programmes, from the call for applications to graduation.

Conformator

Tests open banking APIs against the agreed standard, and issues signed conformance reports.

Sandbox Populator

Fills a sandbox with realistic banks, customers, accounts and transactions in minutes.

OBP Hola

A working third-party app that shows consent, account access and payments end to end.

OBP-Stripe

Takes card payments for API Products through Stripe, and switches access on and off as people pay.
